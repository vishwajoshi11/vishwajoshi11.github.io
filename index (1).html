How I work with data

A simple workflow I use to move from a question to a useful answer.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Define the question

Clarify the problem, target metric and what the analysis needs to answer.

02 — PREPARE

03 — ANALYZE

Find patterns

Use statistics, visualization and models to identify trends and relationships.

04 — COMMUNICATE

Tell the story

Turn findings into clear visuals, dashboards and conclusions that are easy to act on.
